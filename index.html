How to Launch Bodies

Select any celestial body tool from the bottom dock. Click anywhere in the cosmos to position the object, then drag your mouse or finger to define the launch velocity vector. Release to inject it into orbit with real-time trajectory prediction!

🎵

Generative Soundscape

Every celestial body is tuned to harmonic frequencies. As planets reach periapsis and complete resonant orbits, they synthesize crystalline bells, kalimbas, and ethereal chords. Stars project deep sub-bass drones while black holes swallow acoustic waves.

🌌

Spacetime Deformation

Toggle the Grid button to observe general relativistic curvature. The metric coordinate sheet dips dynamically toward massive stars and deep gravitational singularities.

🖐️

Tractor Beam & Eraser

Use the Tractor Beam (W) to drag and slingshot existing planets in real-time. Use the Eraser (X) to cleanly vaporize errant asteroids or rogue stars.
